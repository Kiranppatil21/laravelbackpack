Demo Tips for Success

Before Starting:

  • Ask about their current challenges
  • Understand their business size
  • Identify decision makers present
  • Set expectations for demo duration

During Demo:

  • Focus on pain points they mentioned
  • Use actual numbers from their business
  • Encourage questions throughout
  • Show, don't just tell

After Demo:

  • Recap key benefits specific to their needs
  • Address any concerns raised
  • Provide clear next steps
  • Send follow-up materials

Key Success Factor:

This comprehensive demo script showcases your complete SaaS application from marketing website to deep functionality, emphasizing the business value and competitive advantages at every step. The key is to let the product demonstrate its own value while connecting features to real business problems your client faces.
